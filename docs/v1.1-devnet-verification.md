## Remaining risks

Public fire records and evidence published for previous replays can reveal the likely outcome for another market using the same scenario. The oracle, controller and upgrade authority remain trusted roles. Public Devnet RPC rate limits can slow polling and receipt lookup. Keep the production settlement dataset and evidence store mounted separately from the trading checkout. See [the integrity audit](v1.1-integrity-audit.md) for the operating policy and regression coverage.
